motreader reads and edits Motorola Spectra codeplug archives, the files saved by Spectra RSS (R06.00.05).

Everything runs in your browser. Codeplug files are never uploaded anywhere.

The archive format was reverse-engineered by observing RSS. Work from an archive you have backed up, and check what you write in RSS before programming a radio.

Third-party code: js-yaml (MIT). See vendor/VENDOR.md.
