About motreader

motreader reads and edits Motorola Spectra codeplug archives, and was tested against Spectra RSS (R06.00.05).

Author and Copyright

Elliott H. Liggett, W6EL.

Directions

Import a codeplug and, optionally, your SPECTRAM.DBF. Make your edits and export the codeplug. Place the modified codeplug file in your ARCHIVE directory and load up RSS. Verify the settings you have modified (optional, recommended). Write the modified codeplug to your radio when you're ready.

You may also export the codeplug from motreader to a spreadsheet for further editing: use the "Export CSV" button to do this.

Please note: RSS requires you to keep the codeplug's file name the same as the one it tracks in the SPECTRAM.DBF file. Do not rename your codeplug files; use directories (folders) to keep track of different versions.

Advice

The codeplug format was only discovered through a massive reverse engineering effort, so there may be many corner cases and flat-out errors. Make backups of your codeplugs often. Always verify modified codeplugs in RSS before loading them onto radios. Please send me your codeplugs: doing so makes this software better and more flexible!
